# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Folk Craft is a project for people of all ages. Participants are expected to be respectful, patient and constructive,
to give and accept feedback graciously, and to focus on what is best for the project and its players.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment or discrimination of any kind, insults or personal attacks, sexualized
language or imagery, publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to public spaces when representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they judge inappropriate. Report concerns privately through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/HyrumHendrickson/FolkCraft/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.
